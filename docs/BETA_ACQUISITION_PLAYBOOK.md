# InboxMind beta acquisition playbook

## Goal
Recruit the first 25 genuine beta users across four segments: students, freelancers, indie hackers/founders, and consultants/independent professionals. The offer is free beta access in exchange for honest product feedback.

## Qualification
Prioritize people who personally use Gmail most workdays and can describe a recurring problem involving inbox overload, missed follow-ups, prioritization, or turning messages into tasks. Do not infer need from job title alone.

## Research channels
- Students: university entrepreneurship and productivity communities, student founder clubs, campus tech communities, and public student forums.
- Freelancers: independent-work communities, freelance subreddits and public professional communities.
- Indie hackers: founder communities, build-in-public groups, maker communities and startup forums.
- Consultants: independent consultant communities, solo professional groups and public productivity communities.

Find community rules before posting. Prefer asking moderators for permission, responding to relevant requests, posting a transparent beta invitation where allowed, and opt-in referral links. Do not scrape member lists, harvest personal email addresses, automate unsolicited DMs, or use purchased lists.

## Prospect record fields
Record only public profile/page URL, audience segment, why InboxMind may be relevant, source date, channel rules checked, outreach status, response, consent status, and follow-up date. Do not collect personal emails unless the person publishes them for professional contact and the channel's rules and applicable law permit use. Prefer opt-in signup.

## Message principles
- Identify the builder honestly as an independent developer.
- Say this is an early product and describe only working features.
- Offer free beta access in exchange for feedback.
- Ask one small question or invite them to the signup page.
- Never imply endorsement, existing traction, guaranteed AI accuracy, or public Google OAuth verification.
- Stop contacting anyone who declines or opts out.

## Initial message
Hi — I'm building InboxMind, an early Gmail workspace that helps people spot important messages and keep track of follow-ups. I'm inviting a small group of students, freelancers, founders and independent professionals to try it free and tell me honestly what works and what doesn't. Would you be interested in joining the beta? No pressure either way.

## Follow-up
Thanks for considering it. If useful, here's the beta request page: https://inboxmind-rose.vercel.app/beta/. It's free while we're learning, and the form explains what feedback and contact permission involve.

## Feedback loop
After access is granted, ask users to try one real workflow and submit feedback on onboarding, inbox, priorities, tasks, AI, privacy or bugs. Never ask them to share mailbox content. Track invite → signup → activated → first workflow → feedback → retained at 7 days.

## Metrics
Track qualified conversations, opt-in signups, accepted invites, first successful inbox load, first completed workflow, feedback submissions, 7-day return users, and opt-outs. Report raw counts and date windows; never inflate or extrapolate.

## Email delivery readiness
Resend currently has no verified sender domain. Do not send campaigns until the owner verifies a domain and confirms a sender address. Use email only for people who explicitly requested beta contact or otherwise gave valid permission. Keep one-to-one outreach manually reviewed and compliant with recipient-country requirements.
