InboxMind beta privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice applies to the InboxMind early-access request and feedback forms, not to Gmail mailbox processing inside the InboxMind product. Gmail access is governed by the separate product privacy and Google OAuth disclosures.

What we collect

We collect the email address you submit, optional name, audience category, a short description of your email workflow, your beta feedback commitment, contact consent and consent-version timestamp. If you submit feedback, we also collect its category, rating, text and whether you separately permit an anonymous quote.

Why we use it

  • Review and manage requests for free beta access.
  • Contact you only about your beta request, access and product feedback.
  • Understand product problems and improve InboxMind.
  • Use feedback publicly only when you explicitly permit an anonymous quote.

What we do not do

  • We do not subscribe beta applicants to a marketing newsletter.
  • We do not sell beta applicant data.
  • We do not ask you to paste email contents, client records, passwords or other sensitive mailbox information into the feedback form.

Retention and deletion

Your beta request and feedback are retained while the beta is active and for up to 12 months after the beta closes, unless a shorter period is required. Use the “Delete my beta data” control on the beta page from the browser where you submitted the request. The deletion token is stored in that browser; clearing its site data first may remove your ability to use the control.

Service providers

Signup and feedback data are stored in InboxMind's hosted PostgreSQL database and processed by the InboxMind API hosting provider. Email delivery is not yet enabled for this beta intake form.

Your choices

Submitting the form is optional. You can decline the beta or delete your beta request and feedback using the control described above. Contact consent is limited to beta access and feedback, and is not marketing consent.
